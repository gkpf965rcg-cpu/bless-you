Bless You

For Mac · Always on · On-device

You sneeze.
It says bless you.

A tiny menu bar app that listens while you work. When it hears a sneeze, it says bless you. That is all it does.

Download for Mac

macOS 14 or later · Free · Microphone stays on your Mac

Open in browser Privacy

  1. Download Get the disk image from this page over HTTPS.
  2. Install Open it and drag Bless You into Applications.
  3. Allow the mic Open the app, then allow microphone access when asked.
  4. Sneeze Look for the mic in your menu bar. It keeps listening.

How it tells a sneeze from everything else

Bless You classifies sound on your Mac. It uses Google’s YAMNet model when available — labels that include sneeze and cough — and falls back to an on-device acoustic detector if the model cannot load. Nothing is recorded. Nothing is uploaded. The app has no network access.

It is not perfect. A very theatrical fake sneeze may work. A quiet smothered one might not. Drag the sensitivity slider in the menu bar if it is too eager or too shy.

Audio is classified on your Mac and discarded immediately.
